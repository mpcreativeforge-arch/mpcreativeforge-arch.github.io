MP Cleaner

Privacy Policy
Last updated · 3 October 2026
Your data stays on your device.
MP Cleaner is designed around local processing. Your photos, videos, files and storage information are analyzed directly on your Android device and are not uploaded to MP Creative Forge servers.
01

Developer

MP Cleaner and MP Cleaner PRO are developed by MP Creative Forge.

Contact: mpcreativeforge@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains what information MP Cleaner accesses on your Android device, why it is needed, how it is processed, and what information is not transmitted to us.

02

The short version

MP Cleaner is a local Android storage-management application. Its storage analysis and cleaning tools operate directly on your device.

  • No account is required.
  • No advertising is used by the app.
  • No analytics or behavioral tracking is used.
  • Your photos, videos and files are not uploaded to MP Creative Forge.
  • Storage analysis is performed locally.
  • The app does not automatically delete files.
  • Deletion requires your action and confirmation where required by Android.

Your personal files remain on your device.

03

Information accessed by the app

To provide its features, MP Cleaner may access information stored on your Android device. This information is processed locally.

Photos and videos

MP Cleaner may access photos and videos to identify large media files, screenshots and exact duplicates.

The media itself is not uploaded to MP Creative Forge.

File names, sizes and metadata

The app may read local file names, file sizes, locations and relevant metadata required for storage analysis.

File contents for exact duplicates

When you use Exact Duplicates, MP Cleaner analyzes local file content to determine whether files are genuine copies. This processing takes place on your device.

Installed applications

When Android Usage Access is granted, MP Cleaner can obtain information needed to display application storage usage and last-used information.

Device and battery information

The app may display information such as your device model, Android version, RAM, storage status, battery level, charging status, approximate charging current, temperature and voltage.

App settings

MP Cleaner may store its own settings and preferences locally on your device.

04

How information is used

Information accessed by MP Cleaner is used to provide the features of the application, including:

  • finding large files;
  • finding screenshots;
  • finding exact duplicate files;
  • showing application storage usage;
  • showing last-used application information;
  • showing storage history;
  • scanning folders selected by you;
  • displaying device and battery information;
  • providing storage alerts;
  • providing Charge Guardian notifications;
  • allowing you to select and delete files.

The app does not use your scanned files or media for advertising, profiling or behavioral tracking.

05

Local processing

MP Cleaner is designed around a local-first privacy model. Storage analysis and related processing take place directly on your device.

MP Cleaner does not send your scanned photos, videos, files, file names, duplicate-analysis data or storage information to MP Creative Forge servers.

The app does not require a personal account or cloud storage service for its core storage-analysis features.

06

Permissions

MP Cleaner asks for permissions only when a feature requires them.

Photos and videos

Used to find large media files, screenshots and duplicates.

Usage Access

Optional. Allows MP Cleaner to show real application storage information, last-used dates and related storage information. You grant this access manually through Android system settings.

Notifications

Optional. Used by features such as Charge Guardian and Storage Alert. Notifications are informational only.

Charge Guardian

If enabled, Charge Guardian may use an Android foreground service and related system capabilities to monitor charging status and notify you when selected battery levels are reached.

Charge Guardian does not automatically modify or stop charging your device.

Folder selection

Where supported by Android, MP Cleaner can use the system file picker to let you select specific folders for scanning.

Full file access

MP Cleaner does not request unrestricted access to the entire filesystem.

07

File deletion and safety

MP Cleaner does not automatically delete files.

You decide what should be removed. The app requires user interaction before deletion and uses Android's system confirmation mechanisms where applicable.

You should always review files before confirming deletion.

08

Tracking and advertising

MP Cleaner does not use advertising networks or behavioral advertising.

The app does not use analytics or tracking mechanisms intended to build a profile of how you use the application.

There is no user account and no requirement to register with MP Creative Forge.

09

Data sharing

MP Cleaner does not sell, rent or share your scanned device content with third parties.

Photos, videos, files and storage-analysis information processed by the app remain on your device and are not transmitted to MP Creative Forge.

The core storage-analysis features do not require a third-party cloud processing service.

10

Data retention

Because MP Cleaner does not upload your scanned device content to our servers, MP Creative Forge does not maintain a remote database of your photos, videos, files or storage scans.

Information created by the app for its own operation, such as settings or local storage-history information, may remain on your device until you remove it, clear the application data or uninstall the app.

11

Purchases

MP Cleaner PRO may be purchased through Gumroad.

Payment and transaction processing are handled through Gumroad and its applicable payment infrastructure. MP Cleaner itself does not receive or process your payment card information.

Gumroad's own privacy practices apply to information processed through its website and payment services.

12

Security

MP Cleaner is designed with a local-first privacy model. The application does not create a remote copy of your scanned files for MP Creative Forge.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your Android device, including its screen lock, operating-system security and access to the device.

13

Children's privacy

MP Cleaner is not specifically directed at children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children through remote services.

14

GDPR and your rights

MP Cleaner does not remotely collect or transmit your scanned device content.

If you contact MP Creative Forge by email, we may process the information you choose to provide so that we can respond to your request or provide support.

Where applicable under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you may have rights concerning your personal data, including rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection and data portability where applicable.

For privacy-related requests, contact: mpcreativeforge@gmail.com
