MP Cleaner – Privacy Policy

MP Creative Forge · Last updated: 3 October 2026

This policy applies to MP Cleaner and MP Cleaner PRO ("the app"), developed by MP Creative Forge.

Short version

The app analyzes your storage locally on your device. It does not upload your photos, videos or files, and it does not collect personal data.

What the app does

  • Reads storage information (used and free space) from Android.
  • With your permission, scans photos and videos on your device to find large files, screenshots and exact duplicates. All analysis happens on the device.
  • With your permission (Usage access), reads the storage size of installed apps. The app uses this permission only for that purpose.
  • Deletes files only after you select them and confirm. Android asks for a system confirmation.

What the app does not do

  • It does not upload photos, videos, filenames or any personal files.
  • It does not include advertising or analytics.
  • It does not share user content with third parties.
  • It does not require an account.

Data stored on your device

The app stores only its own settings (language, minimum file size, ignored folders and similar) on your device. This data stays on the device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Purchases

If you buy MP Cleaner PRO on Gumroad, payment and purchase data are handled by Gumroad under its own privacy policy. MP Creative Forge does not receive your payment details through the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy will be updated and the date above changed.
