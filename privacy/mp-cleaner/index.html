Privacy Policy

Transparency matters. This policy explains what MP Cleaner accesses, how it is used, and how your data is handled.

Last updated · 3 October 2026

Developer & contact

Developer: MP Creative Forge

Privacy contact: mpcreativeforge@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy applies to MP Cleaner and MP Cleaner PRO ("the app").

Short version

MP Cleaner is designed to analyze storage locally on your Android device.

The app does not upload your photos, videos, files, filenames or scanned file information to MP Creative Forge or to a remote server.

The app does not require an account and does not use advertising or analytics.

Data the app accesses

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, MP Cleaner may access the following information on your device:

  • Storage information such as used and available storage space, to display storage status.
  • Photos and videos to identify large files, screenshots and exact duplicates.
  • File names and file metadata as necessary to analyze and display files selected by the user.
  • Installed-app storage information when Usage Access permission is granted, solely to display how much storage installed applications use.
  • App preferences such as language, minimum file-size settings and ignored folders.

How data is used

The information accessed by MP Cleaner is used only to provide the app's storage-management features.

  • Finding large files.
  • Identifying screenshots.
  • Identifying exact duplicate files.
  • Displaying storage usage.
  • Displaying storage used by installed applications.
  • Allowing you to select and delete files.

Local processing

File analysis is performed locally on your Android device.

MP Cleaner does not transmit scanned photos, videos, files, filenames or file metadata to MP Creative Forge or to an external server.

No remote database of your personal files is maintained by MP Creative Forge.

What the app does not do

  • It does not upload your photos, videos or personal files.
  • It does not collect or sell personal or sensitive user data.
  • It does not include advertising.
  • It does not include analytics or behavioral tracking.
  • It does not share scanned user content with third parties.
  • It does not require registration or an account.
  • It does not access your location, contacts, microphone or camera for its storage-cleaning functionality.

Permissions

MP Cleaner requests Android permissions only when they are required for a feature you choose to use.

Storage or media access is used to analyze files stored on your device. Usage Access, when enabled, is used only to read storage usage information for installed applications.

You can control or revoke applicable permissions through Android system settings. Some app features may not work without the permissions required for those features.

Security

Because MP Cleaner performs its file analysis locally, the scanned file content is not transmitted to a remote MP Creative Forge server.

The app is designed to minimize data handling and to process only the information required for its stated functionality.

Data retention & deletion

MP Creative Forge does not maintain a remote database containing your scanned files or personal information.

MP Cleaner stores its own application preferences, such as language, minimum file-size settings and ignored folders, locally on your device.

These local preferences are normally removed when the application is uninstalled, subject to Android system behavior and device backup settings.

Files are never deleted automatically as part of the scanning process. A file is deleted only after you select it and confirm the deletion using Android's system controls.

Third-party services

MP Cleaner does not use advertising or analytics services to collect information about users.

If a future version introduces a third-party service that handles user or device data, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the relevant data handling before or when that functionality is introduced, as applicable.

Purchases

MP Cleaner PRO may be offered through Gumroad.

If you purchase MP Cleaner PRO through Gumroad, payment and transaction information is processed by Gumroad according to Gumroad's own terms and privacy practices.

MP Creative Forge does not receive your full payment card or banking details through the MP Cleaner app.

Children

MP Cleaner is not directed specifically at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app, its features or its data practices change.

When changes materially affect privacy, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated accordingly.
