MP Cleaner

Privacy Policy
Last updated: 4 October 2026
Your data stays on your device. MP Cleaner is designed around local processing. The app does not upload your photos, videos, files or scanned storage information to MP Creative Forge.

1. Developer

MP Cleaner and MP Cleaner PRO are developed by MP Creative Forge.

Contact: mpcreativeforge@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses on your Android device, how that information is used locally, and what information is not sent to us.

2. The short version

MP Cleaner is a local Android storage-management application. Its core analysis is performed directly on your device.

  • No account is required.
  • No advertising is used by the app.
  • No analytics or behavioral tracking is used.
  • No cloud processing is used for your scanned content.
  • Your photos, videos and files are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Storage analysis is performed locally on your device.
  • The app does not automatically delete files.
  • Deletion requires user action and confirmation where Android requires it.

Your personal files remain on your device.

3. Information accessed by the app

To provide its features, MP Cleaner may access information stored on your device. This information is processed locally.

Photos and videos

The app may access photos and videos so it can identify large files, screenshots and exact duplicates.

The actual media files are not uploaded to MP Creative Forge.

File names, sizes and metadata

The app may read local file names, file sizes, locations and relevant metadata needed for storage analysis and duplicate detection.

File contents for exact duplicates

When you use the exact duplicate finder, MP Cleaner analyzes local file content to determine whether files are genuine copies. This processing happens on your device.

Installed applications

When you grant Android Usage Access, MP Cleaner can obtain information needed to display application storage usage and last-used information.

Device and battery information

The app may display information such as your device model, Android version, RAM, storage status, battery level, charging status, approximate charging current, temperature and voltage.

App settings

MP Cleaner may store its own settings and preferences locally on your device, for example notification or feature preferences.

4. How information is used

Information accessed by MP Cleaner is used only to provide the features of the app, including:

  • finding large files;
  • finding screenshots;
  • finding exact duplicate files;
  • showing application storage usage;
  • showing last-used application information;
  • showing storage history;
  • scanning folders selected by you;
  • displaying device and battery information;
  • providing storage alerts;
  • providing charge guardian notifications; and
  • allowing you to select and delete files.

The app does not use your scanned files or media for advertising, profiling or behavioral tracking.

5. Local processing

MP Cleaner is designed so that storage analysis and related processing take place on your Android device.

MP Cleaner does not send your scanned photos, videos, files, file names, duplicate-analysis data or storage information to MP Creative Forge servers.

The app does not require a personal account or cloud storage service to perform its core cleaning and analysis functions.

6. Permissions

MP Cleaner asks for permissions only when a feature requires them.

Photos and videos

Used to find large media files, screenshots and duplicates.

Usage Access

Optional. Android Usage Access allows the app to show real application storage information, last-used dates and related storage breakdown information. You grant this access manually through Android system settings.

Notifications

Optional. Used for features such as Charge Guardian and Storage Alert. Notifications are informational only.

Charge Guardian

If enabled, the app may use an Android foreground service and related system capabilities to monitor charging status and notify you when selected battery levels are reached.

Charge Guardian does not automatically modify or stop charging your device.

Full file access

MP Cleaner does not request broad or unrestricted file access. Where Android provides a system file picker, you can choose specific folders for scanning.

7. File deletion and safety

MP Cleaner does not automatically delete files.

You decide what should be removed. The app requires user interaction before deletion and uses Android's system confirmation mechanisms where applicable.

The app is designed to avoid treating Android system components and installed application packages as ordinary removable files.

You should always review files before confirming deletion.

8. Tracking and advertising

MP Cleaner does not use advertising networks or behavioral advertising.

The app does not use analytics or tracking mechanisms intended to build a profile of how you use the application.

There is no user account and no requirement to register with MP Creative Forge.

9. Data sharing

MP Cleaner does not sell, rent or share your scanned device content with third parties.

Photos, videos, files and storage-analysis information processed by the app remain on your device and are not transmitted to MP Creative Forge.

There are no third-party cloud services required for the app's core storage-analysis features.

10. Data retention

Because MP Cleaner does not upload your scanned device content to our servers, MP Creative Forge does not maintain a remote database of your photos, videos, files or storage scans.

Information created by the app for its own operation, such as settings or local storage-history information, may remain on your device until you remove it, clear the application's data, or uninstall the app.

11. Purchases

MP Cleaner PRO may be purchased through Gumroad.

Payment and transaction processing are handled through Gumroad and its applicable payment infrastructure. MP Cleaner itself does not receive or process your payment card information.

Gumroad's own privacy practices apply to information processed through its website and payment services.

12. Security

MP Cleaner is designed with a local-first privacy model. The app does not create a remote copy of your scanned files for MP Creative Forge.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your Android device, including its screen lock, operating-system security and access to the device.

13. Children's privacy

MP Cleaner is not specifically directed at children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children through remote services.

14. GDPR and your rights

MP Cleaner does not remotely collect or transmit your scanned device content. However, privacy and data-protection laws may apply depending on how you use the app and what information you voluntarily provide to us.

If you contact MP Creative Forge by email, we may process the information you choose to provide so that we can respond to your request or provide support.

Where applicable under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you may have rights concerning your personal data, including rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection and data portability where applicable.

For privacy-related requests, contact: mpcreativeforge@gmail.com

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its privacy practices change. The latest version will always be published on this page.

The date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last updated.
