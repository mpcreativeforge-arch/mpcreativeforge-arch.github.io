Privacy Policy

Transparency matters. This policy explains what MP Cleaner accesses, how information is used, and how your data is handled.

Last updated · 4 October 2026

Developer & contact

Developer: MP Creative Forge

Privacy contact: mpcreativeforge@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy applies to MP Cleaner and MP Cleaner PRO (collectively, "the app").

Short version

MP Cleaner is designed to analyze and manage storage locally on your Android device.

The app does not upload your photos, videos, personal files, filenames or scanned file information to MP Creative Forge or to a remote server.

The app does not require an account and does not use advertising, analytics or behavioral tracking.

MP Cleaner is designed to work without cloud processing for its storage-management features.

Data the app accesses

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, MP Cleaner may access information stored or available on your Android device, including:

  • Storage information such as total, used and available storage space.
  • Photos and videos when access is granted, for identifying large files, screenshots and exact duplicates.
  • File names and file metadata required to analyze and display files selected for review.
  • File sizes and file content when required for identifying large files or exact duplicate files.
  • Installed application information required to display application storage usage and related information.
  • Usage information when Android Usage Access has been voluntarily enabled, allowing supported features such as last-used dates and application usage-related data.
  • Device information such as device model, Android version, RAM and storage information, where displayed by the app.
  • App preferences such as language, file-size settings and ignored folders.

How information is used

Information accessed by MP Cleaner is used to provide the storage-management and device-information features of the app.

  • Analyze storage usage.
  • Find large files.
  • Identify screenshots.
  • Identify exact duplicate files using file content.
  • Display installed application storage information.
  • Display supported usage and last-used information.
  • Display device and battery information.
  • Monitor storage status for the optional storage alert.
  • Allow you to select files and confirm their deletion.

Local processing

MP Cleaner performs its storage analysis and file processing locally on your Android device.

Photos, videos, files, filenames, file metadata and duplicate-analysis information are not uploaded to MP Creative Forge or to a remote server by the app.

MP Creative Forge does not maintain a remote database containing your scanned files or their contents.

Internet access is not required for the app's core storage-analysis and cleaning functionality.

What the app does not do

  • It does not upload your photos, videos or personal files.
  • It does not sell your personal or device data.
  • It does not use advertising.
  • It does not use analytics or behavioral tracking.
  • It does not require registration or an account.
  • It does not use cloud processing for storage analysis.
  • It does not share scanned user content with third parties.
  • It does not access your location, contacts, microphone or camera for its storage-management functionality.

Permissions

MP Cleaner requests access only when required by a feature you choose to use.

Android storage or media access may be required to analyze photos, videos and other files on your device.

Usage Access is optional and is used by supported features that display information such as application usage or last-used dates.

You control these permissions through Android system settings. If a permission is denied or revoked, the corresponding feature may be unavailable or may provide limited results.

File deletion & safety

MP Cleaner does not automatically delete files as part of the scanning process.

Files are presented for your review and selection before deletion. Where Android requires system confirmation, the deletion is completed only after the required confirmation is provided.

MP Cleaner is designed to avoid deleting system-critical files or application packages as part of its supported cleaning features.

Security

Because MP Cleaner processes storage information locally, scanned file content is not transmitted to a remote MP Creative Forge server.

The app is designed to minimize data handling and process only the information required for the features you use.

Data retention & deletion

MP Creative Forge does not maintain a remote database containing your scanned files, file contents or personal storage information.

MP Cleaner may store its own application preferences locally on your device, including settings such as language, file-size preferences and ignored folders.

Local application data is normally removed when the app is uninstalled, subject to Android system behavior and any backup mechanisms configured on the device.

MP Cleaner does not retain copies of files that you scan or review on a remote server.

Third-party services

MP Cleaner does not use advertising or analytics services to collect information about users.

The app does not send scanned files, photos, videos, filenames or storage-analysis data to third-party processing services.

If a future version introduces a third-party service that handles user or device data, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the relevant data practices before or when that functionality is introduced, as applicable.

Purchases

MP Cleaner may be distributed as a free version. MP Cleaner PRO is offered through Gumroad as a one-time purchase.

Payment and transaction information for MP Cleaner PRO is processed by Gumroad according to Gumroad's own terms and privacy practices.

The MP Cleaner app does not collect or store your full payment card or banking details.

Purchasing MP Cleaner PRO does not require the app to access your personal files or upload storage content.

Children

MP Cleaner is a general-purpose storage-management application and is not specifically directed at children.

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when MP Cleaner, its features or its data practices change.

When changes materially affect privacy or data handling, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated accordingly.
