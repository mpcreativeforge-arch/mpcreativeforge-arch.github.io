This Privacy Policy explains what information CRYOBYTE CLASH stores, where it is stored and what is never collected.

02The short version

  • CRYOBYTE CLASH is an offline puzzle game. The game uses no servers of its own and sends no data to us. The only connections it may make are to Google Play (purchases) and to Google Play Games (sign-in and cloud save).
  • No account with us and no registration are required. If you use Google Play Games, the game signs in with it automatically; you can play without it.
  • No personal data is collected by MP Creative Forge or MICROVISION s.r.o. Google Play services built into the game process some data on Google's side for purchases and Play Games (see section 08).
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking tools are included.
  • Act I is free. Further acts and CRYO CHIP packs are optional one-time purchases through Google Play. There are no subscriptions.
  • Your settings, progress and records are stored on your device and, if you are signed in to Google Play Games, as a cloud save in your Google account.
  • You can delete all game data at any time.

03Information stored on your device

To provide its features, CRYOBYTE CLASH saves the following information locally, inside the app's private storage on your device:

  • Settings: sound, music, vibration, language, CRT effect, theme and your privacy confirmation.
  • First-launch flag: a small file that remembers the game has been started before, so the intro can be shortened.
  • Game progress: your current level, score and chosen operator, so you can continue where you left off.
  • Records and statistics: best score, best level, total moves, bosses defeated, unlocked achievement cards and badges, the bestiary and the dates they were earned.
  • Inventory and events: CRYO CHIPS, items bought from the in-game trader, and which seasonal events (Halloween, Christmas) you have opened. Chips are earned by playing or bought as optional chip packs through Google Play.
  • Purchase records: the Google Play order numbers of chip packs you bought and the chips they added, so each pack is credited exactly once.

Apart from the cloud save described in section 07, this information does not leave your device, and it is never accessible to MP Creative Forge.

04Information we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or other contact details.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone access.
  • No advertising ID, and no device identifiers sent to us (identifiers used by Google Play services are described in section 08).
  • No usage analytics or crash reports sent to us.

05Permissions

CRYOBYTE CLASH does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage permissions. The only device features it may use are:

  • Vibration: used for haptic feedback during play. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Battery level: read on the device only to show a small battery indicator in the game header. It needs no permission, is not stored and is never sent anywhere.

Apart from Google Play purchases and the Google Play Games cloud save described below, the game does not send any data over the network.

06Purchase through Google Play

Act I of CRYOBYTE CLASH is free. Further acts and CRYO CHIP packs are offered as optional one-time in-app purchases through Google Play. There are no subscriptions. When you buy an act or restore your purchases, the game asks Google Play which acts your Google account owns and unlocks them on your device. Bought chips are added to your in-game wallet. The payment is processed entirely by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy. MP Creative Forge and MICROVISION s.r.o. receive no payment or card details.

07Cloud save and device backup

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, CRYOBYTE CLASH keeps a copy of its local data (settings, progress, records, chips and purchase records) as a saved game in your Google account through Google Play Games Saved Games, so it can be restored after a reinstall or on a new phone. This cloud save is stored by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; MP Creative Forge has no access to it. Google Play Games may show the game your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar); the game does not store or send it. If you do not use Google Play Games, no cloud save is made.

If you have Android device backup turned on, Android may include CRYOBYTE CLASH's local data (settings, progress and records) in your personal device backup, so it can be restored on a new phone. This backup is managed by Android and Google under your Google account settings, not by MP Creative Forge. You can control it in your device's backup settings.

08Data processed by Google, sharing and selling

The game includes the Google Play Billing library and the Google Play Games Services SDK. When you buy something or use Google Play Games, these Google components send the following data to Google, which processes it under Google's Privacy Policy:

  • Purchase history: which products your Google account bought, needed to unlock acts and credit chip packs.
  • App activity: Play Games sign-in and saved-game (cloud save) operations.
  • Device or other IDs: your Play Games player ID and identifiers Google Play services need to run sign-in, purchases and cloud save.

This data is used only to make these features work, is encrypted in transit and is not used for advertising. It is not shared with anyone else, and MP Creative Forge cannot see it beyond the purchase information that Google Play reports to the game on your device.

CRYOBYTE CLASH does not sell, rent, share or transfer any data to third parties. No data is shared with advertisers, analytics providers or other companies.

09Deleting your data

  • Clearing the app's data (storage) in Android settings resets your settings, progress and records.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all CRYOBYTE CLASH data from your device.
  • To delete the cloud save, open the Google Play Games app, go to its settings and delete the data of CRYOBYTE CLASH (or your Play Games account data).

10Your rights (GDPR)

MP Creative Forge does not process your personal data, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. All game data is under your control, on your device and in your own Google account. If you have any questions about privacy or about your rights under the GDPR, contact us at mpcreativeforge@gmail.com.

11Children

CRYOBYTE CLASH is suitable for all ages. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country).

12Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example when new features are added, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date. This policy is also accessible from inside the game, under Settings → PRIVACY POLICY.

Tuto zálohu spravuje Android a Google podle nastavení vašeho účtu Google, nikoli MP Creative Forge. Ovládáte ji v nastavení zálohování svého zařízení.
