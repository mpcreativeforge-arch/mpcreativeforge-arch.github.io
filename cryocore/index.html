01Story

Deep under the ice runs the cryo core that keeps the whole station alive. Its control AI got damaged and started flooding it with its own code. If the board fills up, the core freezes for good.

You are the operator at the last terminal. Line up three or more matching symbols, wipe the code and drain the AI's integrity. It will not make it easy: it sends bosses, lets glitchy creatures loose on the board, triggers disasters and comments on everything you do.
